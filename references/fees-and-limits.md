# Fees, limits, and buyer requirements — Changelly card buys

## What the buyer pays
- Card purchases run through Changelly's third-party payment providers
  (Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, bank transfer depending on region).
- Each provider shows its own fee and limits on the offer screen — the buyer
  picks from a list of offers, best rate on top.
- On top of the provider fee there is a spread over spot price. The spread is
  baked into the quoted rate, not listed as a separate fee line.
- The skill's quote is a CoinGecko **spot estimate before all of this**.
  Always tell the buyer the final numbers appear on Changelly before payment.

## What the buyer must do (on Changelly, never in chat)
- Identity verification (KYC): buying with a card requires ID verification
  with the payment provider — typically ID document plus selfie, a few
  minutes in most cases.
- Enter their own wallet address. If the wallet is an **exchange account**,
  they must also enter the destination tag / memo the exchange requires —
  XRP sent to an exchange without its tag can be lost.
- 18+. Availability varies by country — Changelly shows what is offered for
  the buyer's selected country of residence.

## Practical notes
- Minimum card purchase is provider-dependent (historically around $50 on
  some rails — confirm on the offer screen, do not quote it as fact).
- Crypto is sent to the buyer's own wallet; Changelly does not custody funds.
- Low-value crypto-crypto swaps may skip KYC; **fiat purchases do not**.
